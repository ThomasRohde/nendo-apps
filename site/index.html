Nendo apps in your browser

Every app here runs in your browser, on any computer, with nothing to install. Each one is a whole Nendo file: its screens, its views, Studio and its history, with example data to try.

  • Your changes stay with you. The browser keeps your copy of the app, and only you see it. Nothing you change goes back to this site.
  • Keep your work. Download file in the file menu saves your copy as a .nendo file, which opens in Nendo or in the same app here.
  • Install it. Edge and Chrome offer to install an app, and it then opens in a window of its own, offline too.

Made with an agent

These apps are built to work with a coding agent that Nendo starts beside the file. In the browser there is no agent, so you can look around, open the examples and edit by hand; to ask an agent for more, use them in Nendo.

  • BrandkitsBrand kits: colours, typefaces, logos and samples, with every contrast ratio checked.Open Brandkits
  • SketchboardBoxes-and-arrows diagrams, drafted whole or reworked a part at a time.Open Sketchboard
  • DynamoStock-and-flow models, simulated live as you move the sliders.Open Dynamo
  • CasefileAn investigation wall: claims, sources, evidence for and against, and a brief.Open Casefile
  • DeskHand research and writing to an agent, and review what it delivers.Open Desk

Nendo is a research project, not a supported product. The example data is made up. Prefer the desktop app? nendo-apps on GitHub has every app as a .nendo file, and says how to get Nendo.
